WEAPONS

Build your loadout in the ARMORY: three primaries, two secondaries, a melee weapon and grenades, used in every mode. 128 catalogue weapons are available to unlock with your level or with kills and headshots in their class. Optics, muzzle devices, grips, lasers, lights and magazines can be changed per weapon. With a red dot or holographic sight the reticle sits exactly on the point of aim; magnified scopes (3.5x and up) switch to a full scope view. A laser marks the exact impact point - the beam shows at night and in dark places. Drums and barrels explode when shot and set off the ones next to them.

TEAMS

A red arrow and name mark every hostile, a blue arrow your squad (visible through walls). Under night vision hostiles glow red and your squad blue. Elite commandos appear more often on harder difficulties - open INTEL for the threat ranking.

MODES

CAMPAIGN twelve story missions with step-by-step objectives.

TEAM DEATHMATCH BRAVO squad against hostile commandos: kills, the moving HARDPOINT, the marked HVT and dog tags all score; killstreaks give a UAV sweep, a resupply and an airstrike.

SURVIVAL rounds of assaults with limited ammo and half-health regeneration. Every round has a field objective; extraction can be called every 5th round.

ENDLESS ever stronger waves with infinite ammo - launchers, sniper / scoped rifles and grenades excepted: they get a small random supply every game (also in TEAM DEATHMATCH).

TIPS

Stay in cover and peek - Top Class and Legendary hostiles flank, dive out of your fire and rush you while you reload. Switch on night vision (N) when the HUD recommends it.
